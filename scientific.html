Scientific Foreknowledge

The Qur'an is not a science textbook and does not claim to be one. But it contains descriptions of natural phenomena — stated in specific Arabic terms with specific meanings — that match what modern science has confirmed centuries later. The question is not whether the Qur'an teaches science. The question is: how did an unlettered man in 7th-century Arabia describe embryological stages, deep-sea conditions, cosmic expansion, and the extraterrestrial origin of iron — without error?

What counts as foreknowledge

Not every overlap between the Qur'an and modern science counts. For a description to qualify as foreknowledge on this page, it must meet all three criteria:

  1. The Arabic text is specific — not a vague metaphor that can be stretched to mean anything, but a term with a defined semantic range.
  2. The knowledge was unavailable — not part of common observation or inherited Greek/Persian learning accessible in 7th-century Arabia.
  3. Modern science confirms it — the description matches current scientific understanding, not an outdated theory.

Where any of these criteria is debatable, it is noted.

Human embryological development

ConfirmedQur'anic
وَلَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ مِن سُلَـٰلَةٍ مِّن طِينٍ ۝ ثُمَّ جَعَلْنَـٰهُ نُطْفَةً فِى قَرَارٍ مَّكِينٍ ۝ ثُمَّ خَلَقْنَا ٱلنُّطْفَةَ عَلَقَةً فَخَلَقْنَا ٱلْعَلَقَةَ مُضْغَةً فَخَلَقْنَا ٱلْمُضْغَةَ عِظَـٰمًا فَكَسَوْنَا ٱلْعِظَـٰمَ لَحْمًا ثُمَّ أَنشَأْنَـٰهُ خَلْقًا ءَاخَرَ ۚ فَتَبَارَكَ ٱللَّهُ أَحْسَنُ ٱلْخَـٰلِقِينَ
"And certainly We created man from an extract of clay. Then We placed him as a drop (nutfah) in a firm resting place. Then We made the drop into a clinging structure ('alaqah), then We made the clinging structure into a chewed-like lump (mudghah), then We made from the lump bones, then We clothed the bones with flesh, then We brought it into being as another creation. So blessed is Allah, the best of creators."
Qur'an 23:12-14

Each Arabic term describes a specific stage that matches modern embryology:

StageArabicMeaningModern confirmation
NutfahنُطْفَةA small drop; the fertilized ovumZygote — a single cell from male and female gametes
'AlaqahعَلَقَةThree meanings: (1) leech, (2) something that clings, (3) blood clotDays 7-24: the embryo implants (clings) to the uterine wall, resembles a leech in shape, and is surrounded by blood
MudghahمُضْغَةA chewed substance — something bearing teeth marksDays 24-28: the somites along the embryo's back give it the exact appearance of a chewed piece of gum, with visible indentations
'IẓāmعِظَـٰمBonesWeek 6-7: cartilaginous skeletal framework forms
LaḥmلَحْمFlesh/muscle coveringWeek 7-8: muscles develop and wrap around the skeletal framework

The عَلَقَة ('alaqah) stage is particularly striking. The word carries three simultaneous meanings — all three of which are accurate descriptions of the embryo at days 7-24:

  • Leech — the embryo at this stage physically resembles a leech in shape
  • Something that clings — the embryo implants and clings to the uterine wall
  • Blood clot — the embryo is surrounded by blood at the implantation site

No 7th-century observer could have known the shape of a human embryo at day 15. The earliest accurate embryological illustrations date from the 15th century. The Qur'anic description preceded scientific confirmation by over 800 years.

The Galenic objection: Critics argue that Galen described embryological stages and Muhammad ﷺ could have learned from Greek medicine. But Galen described the embryo as forming from menstrual blood (incorrect), assigned different stages, and had no concept of implantation. The Qur'anic description is not a copy of Galenic embryology — it corrects it where it was wrong and describes what Galen could not have known (the leech-like clinging stage, the chewed appearance of somites).

Deep-sea darkness and internal waves

Confirmed
أَوْ كَظُلُمَـٰتٍ فِى بَحْرٍ لُّجِّىٍّ يَغْشَىٰهُ مَوْجٌ مِّن فَوْقِهِۦ مَوْجٌ مِّن فَوْقِهِۦ سَحَابٌ ۚ ظُلُمَـٰتٌ بَعْضُهَا فَوْقَ بَعْضٍ
"Or as darkness in a deep sea, covered by waves, above which are waves, above which are clouds — darknesses one above another."
Qur'an 24:40

This verse describes three phenomena confirmed by 20th-century oceanography:

  1. Deep-sea darkness — بَحْرٍ لُّجِّىٍّ (baḥr lujjī) means a deep, vast sea. Below 200 meters, there is almost no sunlight. Below 1,000 meters, it is pitch dark. No 7th-century human had dived to these depths.
  2. Internal waves — مَوْجٌ مِّن فَوْقِهِۦ مَوْجٌ — "waves, above which are waves." Internal waves occur at the density interface between different ocean layers (the thermocline/pycnocline). They were not discovered until the 20th century. The description of waves beneath waves is only physically meaningful if internal ocean waves exist.
  3. Layered darkness — ظُلُمَـٰتٌ بَعْضُهَا فَوْقَ بَعْضٍ — "darknesses one above another." As light penetrates the ocean, different wavelengths are absorbed at different depths — red disappears first, then orange, yellow, green, and finally blue. The darkness is indeed layered.

Arabia is a desert peninsula. The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ never crossed a deep ocean. The description of layered deep-sea conditions — internal waves, progressive darkness — is not something a 7th-century Arabian could have observed or learned from available sources.

Iron "sent down" from the sky

Confirmed
وَأَنزَلْنَا ٱلْحَدِيدَ فِيهِ بَأْسٌ شَدِيدٌ وَمَنَـٰفِعُ لِلنَّاسِ
"And We sent down iron, in which is great might and benefits for mankind."
Qur'an 57:25

The verb أَنزَلْنَا (anzalnā) means "We sent down" — the same verb used for the revelation of scripture. For iron, this is scientifically precise: iron is not formed on Earth. The temperatures required for iron nucleosynthesis (~6 billion degrees) only exist in the cores of massive stars. When these stars explode as supernovae, they scatter iron across space. The iron on Earth was literally sent down from the sky — delivered via meteorite impacts during the late heavy bombardment ~4 billion years ago.

Additionally, Surah Al-Hadid (The Iron) is the 57th surah. The atomic number of iron is 26. The isotope Fe-57 is one of the four stable isotopes of iron. While this numerical observation is suggestive rather than formally structured like the word-count patterns, it is noted here as a curiosity.

The expanding universe

Confirmed
وَٱلسَّمَآءَ بَنَيْنَـٰهَا بِأَيْيدٍ وَإِنَّا لَمُوسِعُونَ
"And the heaven We constructed with strength, and indeed, We are its expander."
Qur'an 51:47

لَمُوسِعُونَ (la-mūsi'ūn) is an active participle from the root و-س-ع (to expand, to make vast). The active participle form indicates an ongoing, continuous action — "We are [continuously] expanding it." This is not past tense ("We expanded it") but present-continuous.

Cosmic expansion was discovered by Edwin Hubble in 1929 — over 1,300 years after this verse. Before Hubble, the scientific consensus (including Einstein's original formulation) assumed a static universe. The Qur'an's description of ongoing expansion is consistent with — and only with — the post-Hubble understanding of cosmology.

Further confirmed descriptions

The barrier between salt and fresh water

مَرَجَ ٱلْبَحْرَيْنِ يَلْتَقِيَانِ ۝ بَيْنَهُمَا بَرْزَخٌ لَّا يَبْغِيَانِ
"He released the two seas meeting side by side; between them is a barrier neither of them transgresses."
Qur'an 55:19-20

The halocline — a density gradient between salt and fresh water at estuaries — keeps them visually and physically distinct. They meet but do not fully mix due to differences in salinity and density. This phenomenon is confirmed by modern oceanography.

Mountains as pegs with deep roots

أَلَمْ نَجْعَلِ ٱلْأَرْضَ مِهَـٰدًا ۝ وَٱلْجِبَالَ أَوْتَادًا
"Have We not made the earth as a wide expanse, and the mountains as pegs?"
Qur'an 78:6-7

أَوْتَاد (awtād) means pegs or stakes — objects with a visible portion above ground and a larger portion driven into the ground. Mountains have deep roots extending into the Earth's mantle (isostasy), discovered by George Airy in 1855. The analogy is geologically precise: the hidden root is far larger than the visible peak.

Unique fingerprints

أَيَحْسَبُ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنُ أَلَّن نَّجْمَعَ عِظَامَهُۥ ۝ بَلَىٰ قَـٰدِرِينَ عَلَىٰٓ أَن نُّسَوِّىَ بَنَانَهُۥ
"Does man think that We cannot assemble his bones? Yes, We are able — even to proportion his fingertips."
Qur'an 75:3-4

In the context of resurrection — Allah can reassemble even the most individually unique part of the body. Why specify fingertips? Because fingerprints are unique to every individual. This was not scientifically recognized until Sir William Herschel's work in the 1850s-1880s. The Qur'an highlights fingertips as the ultimate demonstration of reconstructive power — which only makes sense if they carry unique identifying information.

Pain receptors in the skin

إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا بِـَٔايَـٰتِنَا سَوْفَ نُصْلِيهِمْ نَارًا كُلَّمَا نَضِجَتْ جُلُودُهُم بَدَّلْنَـٰهُمْ جُلُودًا غَيْرَهَا لِيَذُوقُوا ٱلْعَذَابَ
"Those who disbelieve in Our signs — We will drive them into a Fire. Every time their skins are roasted through, We will replace them with other skins so they may taste the punishment."
Qur'an 4:56

Pain receptors (nociceptors) are concentrated in the skin. When skin is fully burned (third-degree burns), pain sensation is lost because the nerve endings are destroyed. The Qur'an specifies that skins are replaced so they may taste the punishment — linking the skin specifically to the sensation of pain. Modern dermatology confirms that pain perception requires intact skin nerve endings.

All life from water

وَجَعَلْنَا مِنَ ٱلْمَآءِ كُلَّ شَىْءٍ حَىٍّ ۖ أَفَلَا يُؤْمِنُونَ
"And We made from water every living thing. Then will they not believe?"
Qur'an 21:30

Every known form of life requires water. The cytoplasm of all cells is water-based. No life process occurs without water. The statement is absolute — كُلَّ شَىْءٍ حَىٍّ (every living thing) — and it has not been falsified by any biological discovery.

What this page does not claim

  • The Qur'an is not a science textbook. Its purpose is guidance, not scientific instruction.
  • Not every verse with a natural description constitutes "scientific foreknowledge." Some descriptions are observational and were available to anyone.
  • The scientific confirmations are part of the cumulative case — not standalone proofs. This domain works together with the six other domains to build the constraint satisfaction argument.
  • The Arabic words sometimes carry a range of meanings, and scholarly interpretation varies. Where the range is wide, this page notes it.
