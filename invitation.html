The Invitation

This evidence was not compiled to amaze you. It was compiled because every person reading this page will die — and no one knows when. And after death, there is no second chance, no revision, no additional time to investigate.

What Islam is

Islam is not a cultural identity, a political movement, or a set of inherited traditions. Islam is submission to the One God — the voluntary surrender of your will to the will of the One who created you, who sustains you every moment, and to whom you will return.

The core of Islam is the shahādah — the testimony of faith:

أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ اللهِ
"I bear witness that there is no god worthy of worship except Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah."

This is all it takes to become Muslim. One sincere declaration. Not a lengthy process, not a ceremony requiring clergy, not a conversion that takes months. One statement, made with conviction, and you stand before Allah as a Muslim — all previous sins forgiven, a new beginning.

Islam rests on five pillars:

  1. The Shahādah — Testifying that there is no god but Allah and that Muhammad ﷺ is His final Messenger.
  2. Ṣalāh — Five daily prayers, connecting the servant directly to the Creator. No intermediary, no clergy required.
  3. Zakāh — Giving a fixed portion of wealth to those in need. Purification of wealth and soul.
  4. Ṣawm — Fasting during Ramadan. Discipline, empathy, and gratitude.
  5. Ḥajj — Pilgrimage to Makkah once in a lifetime, if able. Equality before Allah — every person in the same white garment, no distinction of wealth or status.

And six articles of faith:

  1. Belief in Allah — one God, without partners, without offspring, without likeness.
  2. Belief in the Angels — created beings who carry out Allah's commands.
  3. Belief in the Books — the Torah, the Psalms, the Gospel (in their original forms), and the Qur'an as the final, preserved revelation.
  4. Belief in the Messengers — from Adam to Muhammad ﷺ, including Abraham, Moses, and Jesus, peace be upon them all.
  5. Belief in the Last Day — the Day of Judgment, when every person will be held accountable.
  6. Belief in Divine Decree — that Allah's knowledge encompasses all things, and that He is the ultimate source of all that occurs.

The urgency

You did not choose to be born. You cannot choose when you will die. Between those two events, you have one opportunity to examine the evidence, make a decision, and act on it. That opportunity is now.

Every second that passes is a second closer to the moment when examination will no longer be possible. The person who dies at 80 and the person who dies tonight face the same question: did you respond to the signs when they were shown to you?

حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءَ أَحَدَهُمُ ٱلْمَوْتُ قَالَ رَبِّ ٱرْجِعُونِ ۝ لَعَلِّىٓ أَعْمَلُ صَـٰلِحًا فِيمَا تَرَكْتُ ۚ كَلَّآ ۚ إِنَّهَا كَلِمَةٌ هُوَ قَآئِلُهَا ۖ وَمِن وَرَآئِهِم بَرْزَخٌ إِلَىٰ يَوْمِ يُبْعَثُونَ
"Until, when death comes to one of them, he says: 'My Lord, send me back, that I may do righteousness in that which I left behind.' No! It is only a word he is saying, and behind them is a barrier until the Day they are resurrected."

كَلَّا — "No!" There is no going back. There is no second chance. There is no "I'll think about it later." There is only this life, and then the accounting.
