What this site is — and what it is not

This is a structured corpus of evidence for the divine authorship of the Qur'an. Every page uses only the Qur'an itself as the primary source — no hadith literature is used as evidence. Historical facts are cited where the Qur'an makes claims about observable reality (such as the preservation promise or the unmet literary challenge), but the evidence chain begins and ends with the Qur'anic text.

This site is not an emotional appeal. It is not a collection of "amazing facts." It is a systematic, domain-by-domain examination of a single question: could a human being have produced this Book?

Every claim on this site is labeled by what it is:

  • Verified — A fact reproducible from a pinned public corpus. Count it yourself.
  • Confirmed — A description that matches modern scientific or historical findings.
  • Standing — A challenge or claim that remains unrefuted after centuries.
  • Rejected — A popular claim that this project tested and found to be false.

The conclusion that the Qur'an is the word of Allah is a conviction, argued openly from the evidence — never smuggled into the data layer.
